About Peace SMP

Peace SMP was created to escape the pay-to-win culture that dominates most Minecraft survival servers. Here, everyone starts equal and plays the game the way it was meant to be played.

🌿

Semi-Vanilla

Classic survival with a light touch. Exciting improvements are on the way.
🕊️

Peaceful Community

A friendly, welcoming player base that values respect and cooperation.
🗺️

No Map Resets

Your builds are permanent. We never reset the map, so your legacy lasts.
🚫

No Pay-to-Win

No paid advantages. Progress is earned through play, not purchases.
🛡️

Land claim

Protects your area from players and creepers after you claim the land.
🏠

Sethome

Set a home using /sethome so you can go back if you are lost.
📋

Whitelist Feature

Our server supports cracked accounts, so we use a whitelist to keep unauthorized accounts out and avoid problems. We value the quality of our players over quantity.
