About Peace SMP

Peace SMP was built to offer a genuine, non-toxic Minecraft experience without pay-to-win mechanics. Every player starts on equal footing.

🌿

Semi-Vanilla

Classic survival gameplay with tasteful quality-of-life enhancements and exciting community features.
🕊️

Peaceful Community

A warm, friendly, and respectful environment where players collaborate on gigantic projects.
🗺️

No Map Resets

Your builds, farms, and megaprojects are permanent. We never reset maps, preserving your legacy forever.
🚫

No Pay-to-Win

Zero paid ranks that give gameplay advantages. Everything is unlocked through active play and dedication.
🛡️

Land Claim

Simple claim protection keeps your builds safe from other players and rogue creeper explosions.
🏠

Set Home

Convenient /sethome commands allow you to explore far away and teleport back safely anytime.
📋

Whitelist Protected

Our server supports both premium and offline-mode accounts. We maintain a whitelist to keep griefers out and ensure high community standards.
